## 🌼 About Study Quest

**Study Quest** is an automated study tool designed to help students stay organized without the stress of manually planning everything.

It automatically creates a **personalized study schedule** based on:

- 🗓 **Your class schedule**
- 📚 **The subjects you want or need to study**
- ⏳ **Your study commitment** (how long you can study per day)

With just a few inputs, Study Quest generates a study plan that **fits perfectly into your real-life routine**. No complicated setup — just clarity and structure. ✨

You can also:

- 📂 **Upload your study materials** and keep everything neatly organized  
- ✏️ **Adjust study sessions** anytime  
- 🌸 View all tasks and resources in a clean, calming interface  

Study Quest aims to make studying simpler, lighter, and more manageable — giving you structure without the overwhelm. 💗
